In one sentence

One More Round collects no personal information whatsoever. Everything you do in the app

(rounds played, scores, favourites, level, achievements) stays on your own phone —

because this app has no server at all.

1. What we collect

1.1 What we do NOT collect

One More Round does not collect, read, or upload any of the following:

  • ❌ Names, phone numbers, email addresses, ID numbers, or any personal identifiers
  • ❌ Contacts, SMS, or call logs
  • ❌ Photos, videos, microphone, or camera
  • ❌ Location (GPS or network-based)
  • ❌ Device identifiers (IMEI, OAID, Android ID, advertising ID)
  • ❌ Installed-app lists or clipboard contents
  • ❌ Any usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports

    • 1.2 Where your game data lives

    Your level, XP, completion records, favourites, achievements and settings

    (language / accent colour / layout mode) are stored **locally in the app's private

    directory on your device** (HarmonyOS preferences storage).

  • This data **never leaves your phone**
  • **Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently** — we keep no copy and cannot restore it
  • We have **no ability** to read it (no server, no accounts, no cloud sync)

    • 2. Permissions we request, and why

    The app declares three permissions in module.json5. Every one has a specific purpose,

    and none is used to collect information:

    PermissionPurposeWhen it is used

    |---|---|---|

    `ohos.permission.INTERNET`**Only for LAN multiplayer** — direct device-to-device communication with another device on the same Wi-Fi (UDP broadcast)**Only when you host or join a room**
    `ohos.permission.GET_WIFI_INFO`Reads the current Wi-Fi name to check whether two devices are on the same network, so LAN play can connectSame as above
    `ohos.permission.VIBRATE`A short vibration on a correct answer or when a game ends (can be turned off in Settings)During gameplay

    ⚠️ A note about "network access"

  • **This app does not connect to any internet server.** There is no backend, no API, no cloud service.
  • The `INTERNET` permission is used **solely for local device-to-device communication** (within one Wi-Fi network).
  • **If you never start a LAN match, no feature of this app uses the network at all.**
  • **In aeroplane mode, all 355 games work normally.**

    • 3. Third-party SDKs

    This app includes no third-party SDKs.

  • ❌ No advertising SDK (the app contains **no ads**)
  • ❌ No analytics SDK (e.g. Umeng, Sensors, Google Analytics)
  • ❌ No crash-reporting SDK (e.g. Bugly, Firebase Crashlytics)
  • ❌ No push-notification SDK
  • ❌ No social-sharing SDK

    • 4. Children's privacy

    This app collects information from no one, including children.

    Its content is puzzle-oriented (numbers, logic, words, memory, reaction) —

    no violence, no horror, no social features, no in-app purchases — and is suitable for all ages.

    Parents with questions are welcome to contact us at the address below.

    5. Data security

  • Your data **exists only on your own device** ⇒ there is **no risk of interception in transit

    • or of a server being breached**

  • LAN matches transmit **only the current game state** (e.g. a move, or a race finish time):

    • - sent by UDP broadcast directly to the other device on the same local network

    - never through any relay server

    - discarded when the match ends — never written to disk, never uploaded

  • The app **does not request storage permissions** and cannot read any of your files

    Because we collect none of your information, the following rights hold by construction:

    RightHow it is satisfied
